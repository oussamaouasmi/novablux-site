Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Nova Blux mobile game ("Nova Blux", "the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

Information we do not collect

Nova Blux has no accounts or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location, and the app sends no data to servers operated by us.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, best levels and settings (sound, music, vibration, theme) are stored only on your device. If Android backup or iCloud backup is turned on, the operating system may include this data in your own backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. Because we never receive this data, we cannot see, restore or delete it for you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Nova Blux is free and shows banner ads, and optional video ads you can choose to watch to continue a game, provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or iOS IDFA, where permitted);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped, and diagnostic information.

This information is collected and used by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your choices and consent

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: Settings > Privacy & Terms > Ad Privacy Choices.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). On iOS the app asks for tracking permission, which you can change in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • If you decline personalised ads you may still see non-personalised ads.

Children

Nova Blux is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are limited to family-friendly content. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app or its data. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policies above; you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. As we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through Google's tools above, but you can always contact us and we will respond within 30 days. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page and in the app with a new effective date.
